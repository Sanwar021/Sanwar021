## 👋 About Me

I am **Sanwar Islam**, a **Machine Learning Engineer, AI Researcher, and Full-Stack Developer** from Dhaka, Bangladesh.

My work focuses on **Computer Vision, Medical AI, Deep Learning, and real-world healthcare technology**. I have published **9 research papers** across **IEEE, Springer, and SCOPUS-indexed venues**, and I am currently building **Upasham Prescription**, a modern medical platform powered by full-stack engineering and AI-assisted healthcare workflows.
